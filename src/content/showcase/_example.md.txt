This file is not picked up by the collection (it's *.md.txt, not *.md).
It's just a copy-pasteable template for adding a new showcase item.

Copy this into a new file named something like `my-new-item.md` in this
same folder, fill it in, and it will automatically appear in the homepage
Featured Showcase (sorted by `order`, then `date`, newest first).

---
title: "Your item's title"
type: "achievement"   # one of: article | project | research | achievement | launch | update
summary: "One short editorial sentence, ~90 characters or less."
date: 2026-10-01       # optional
href: "/writing/some-post"   # internal route or full external URL
cta: "See the details"       # optional, defaults to something sensible per `type`
order: 3                     # optional, lower numbers show first
---
